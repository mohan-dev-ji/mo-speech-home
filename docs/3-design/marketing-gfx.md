# Marketing GFX material
Mo Speech needs marketing material in the form of motion graphics

## The problem
AAC technology inherently appeals to the visual nature of humans. With expressive themes and a simpler layout to regular AAC software - Mo Speech is built on this fact to give autistic people more of what they want in order to bind a personal attachment to their device. This has to shine through in the marketing material.

## Proposed solution
We intend to build high quality graphics and animations that capture the importance of the  problems in current AAC systems that Mo Speech tackles head on. We plan to use the UI designs from figma, the bg animations from pro & max themes and screen recording of the app in action as source material. 

This process will involve the creation of 3d animations in blender that show the UI being built from various elements and components. Here we can get deep into the atoms of the UI where the 3d animations show them coming together in a creative way in high-end captivating motion graphics for a number of use case. These animations can act as opening bumpers for explainer videos, graphical inserts for promotional material or even self contained short form content highlighting a specific component or feature. For this reason it will be a good idea to render out 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 finals.

Even though this project is for the graphics material for marketing - it also spills into the domain of the other marketing content on the agenda. A series of explainer screen recordings will be made to show users how to use the app and what is possible. Along side this narrated marketing blurbs will be recorded which and used for edited videos that combine the 3d animations, screen recordings and other graphical assets to form well edited promotional videos perfect for ads and social media posts.

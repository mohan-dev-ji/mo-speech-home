# Mo Speech: the product

## In one line

**Mo Speech is an AAC app for families: a child's voice, in their own language,
set up and protected by the people who care for them.**

## The problem

AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) gives a voice to children who
can't rely on speech, many of them autistic. Families run into the same walls
again and again:

- **The setup gets scrambled.** AAC users are often ingenious at rearranging
  their boards. A carefully built setup is undone in minutes, and the adult is
  left unable to get it back.
- **It only speaks one language well.** Most tools are built for English. A
  family that speaks Hindi at home is left translating a child's world into
  someone else's language.
- **It's built for one kind of learner.** Most boards assume language is built
  word by word. Many autistic children learn in **whole phrases first**
  (gestalt language processing) and break them down later. A word-only board
  doesn't meet them where they are.
- **The pictures aren't theirs.** A generic symbol for "cup" isn't *their* cup.
  Personal photos make AAC meaningful, but most tools make them hard to add.
- **It's expensive, and it's one device.** Dedicated devices cost a lot, and
  what's set up at home rarely matches anything else.

## What Mo Speech is

A web app for tablets and phones, used at home by a family and one or more
students:

- **A symbol board that stays put.** Coloured categories of professional
  SymbolStix symbols that speak when tapped, kept in place so a child learns
  where things are.
- **Sentences, the way children actually learn them.** A talker that builds
  sentences from single words **and** whole phrases, and plays them block by
  block or fluently, with feeling.
- **Lists and routines.** Step-by-step picture schedules, with First-Then and
  checklists.
- **An instructor view and a student view.** The adult shapes what each child
  sees, opens it up as they grow, and locks it so the setup stays intact.
- **Their own pictures and voices.** Photos, Creative Commons images or AI
  drawings, and recordings in a parent's own voice.
- **Language first.** The whole app, every board and every voice in the
  child's language: English, Hindi and Spanish today, with more on the way.
- **Ready-made content.** Boards, routines and sentences made by Mo Speech,
  added in one tap.

## What makes it different

1. **Built for gestalt learners as well as word-by-word learners.** Phrases are
   first-class, and a sentence can be heard in its parts or as a whole.
2. **The instructor's setup is protected.** Views, permissions and a lock keep
   the child's board as the adult intended.
3. **The home language is the main language, not a translation.** Content,
   sentences and phrases are made properly for each language, with the right
   words and word order, and each language has its own voices.
4. **Free to start, properly.** A real free plan with no trial clock, so a child
   can start communicating today.

## Principles

These guide every decision. When in doubt, they win.

- **Motor memory is sacred.** Nothing on a student's board moves unless an
  adult deliberately moves it.
- **The instructor stays in control.** A student gets exactly what their
  instructor allows, and no more, until the instructor decides otherwise.
- **Every tap speaks.** No symbol is ever silent. If there's no recording, the
  words are read aloud.
- **Language is structure, not decoration.** Sentences and phrases get a real
  version per language, never a word-for-word swap.
- **Children's data is protected by design.** Nothing a child says is ever
  collected. Analytics are anonymous and can be switched off.
- **Honest pricing.** Free is for using Mo Speech, Pro for shaping it, and Max
  for going beyond the symbol library.
